## Phase Goal

**MVP note (MVP_MODE=true):** the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for Phase 8 reads *"A CopilotKit surface renders a heterogeneous commitment ledger, proving generative UI"* — outcome-shaped, but not in `As a / I want to / so that` form. <objective>
Prove CopilotKit can talk to Kilo Gateway at all, then land the two deterministic halves the ledger surface depends on: the additive `commitment` intent type (STR-04) and the pure row-to-component selector plus its hand-seeded demo rows.

This plan leads with the risk gate. 08-RESEARCH Open Question 1 (CopilotKit/CopilotKit#3317 — the second request in a chat session mis-routed to Kilo Gateway's `/responses` endpoint) and Assumption A2 (constructor shapes at exactly 1.71.0) are both unresolved, and either can eat the entire 45–60 minute box. Task 1 answers both empirically in minutes and is the abandon signal: if CopilotKit cannot complete one tool-calling round trip against Kilo Gateway within about 15 minutes, abandon the phase per D-02 — no merge, no further time. Tasks 2 and 3 are then insulated from CopilotKit entirely; both are verified by hand-run scripts, not by the chat UI.

Plan split per D-14: 08-01 is the commitment extraction type plus component selection logic (led by the smoke-test tracer); 08-02 is the full ledger surface plus nudge wiring, and runs after this plan (wave 2). D-04: this phase runs alongside nothing — one executor, never in parallel with Phase 9 or Phase 10.

Purpose: STR-04's check ("the schema change works") and STR-05's check ("two questions render two visibly different components on stage") have different failure modes. Deliberately decoupled — STR-04 is proven by a hand-run script, never by wiring the ledger to live extraction.

Output: three CopilotKit packages at exact pins, one additive export in `lib/ai/provider.ts`, the runtime route, a bare ledger surface, the commitment schema and extractor, and the selector plus seed rows.
</objective>
