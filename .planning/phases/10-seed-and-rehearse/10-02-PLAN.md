## Rehearsal card (defaults; Task 1 confirms or adjusts them from Phase 7 artifacts)

| Beat | Who | Exact action (never mention the bot) | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chatter | B | post `haha yeah that thread yesterday was a mess` | No card; an ignored Decision shows on the dashboard |
| 11:00 ask | B | post `Let's have a talk next Friday at 11am.` | Approval card for Fri 18 Sep 2026 11:00 HKT |
| Approve | A (only A holds Google consent, Phase 4 D-08) | click Approve | Card confirms in place with calendar and Meet links; event appears on A's calendar |
| 10:30 ask | B | post `Also need about an hour with you next Friday at 10:30am.` | Conflict card with two reasoned alternatives, or the CFL-05 warning |
| Pick alternative | A (alternatives mode only) | click one alternative | Card confirms; a second event appears at the chosen slot |

Why the 10:30 text reads this way:
- It follows the orchestrator's suggestion ("Also need about an hour with you at 10:30 that same day"), but replaces "that same day" with "next Friday". Phase 5 D-08 extracts each message as an array of one, so "that same day" has no date to anchor to.
- Stating an hour makes the request end at 11:30. Under the half-open overlap rule it clashes with the 11:00 event regardless of the default duration (07-RESEARCH Pitfall 1).
- Task 1 switches to Phase 7's proven dry-run text if that text names the day and a duration longer than 30 minutes.

"next Friday" means Fri 18 Sep 2026 only when the rehearsal runs on Sat 12 Sep 2026 HKT; Task 1 checks the date. Stale bot cards from earlier runs stay in channel history. Cleaning them is the cosmetic Claude's-discretion item that is cut first, so each run is judged on its newest cards only.
