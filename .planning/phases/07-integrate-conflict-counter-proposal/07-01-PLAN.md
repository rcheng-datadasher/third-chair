<assumption_delta_decision>
noun: the Proposal's current `start`/`end`
decision: promote
rationale: The detector fired on `chosen` ("choosing an alternative slot", "the chosen slot") and on `alternative`. This is a real modelling shift and it lands in 07-02, so it is decided here for the whole phase. Before this phase, a Proposal's `start`/`end` were *derived* — whatever extraction resolved from the message. From 07-02 on they are *chosen* — extraction supplies only the initial value and a click can replace it. The decision is to **promote** "whatever `start`/`end` currently sit on the row" to the single source of truth the approve path reads, and to **demote** "the slot the message asked for" to merely that field's first value. Concretely: the `choose_alt` handler updates `start`/`end` on the row and then calls the existing approve function unchanged (D-15), rather than adding a slot parameter to the approve path. No schema change, no second identity: one Proposal still owns exactly one slot at any moment, and `alternatives Json?` is a staging field that is written once and read once. Accepted debt, flagged: a pending Proposal carrying alternatives counts as a busy block in later clash checks until it is resolved — 07-02 Task 1 excludes exactly those rows from the union (07-RESEARCH Pitfall 5), which is the resolution chosen over a schema change. What would force a later promote to a separate `ProposalSlot` table: supporting more than two alternatives, or keeping a history of offered-but-rejected slots.
